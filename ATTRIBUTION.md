# Attribution and data provenance

The implementation in `src/vj_nca` is original code, informed by the papers linked in
[docs/RESEARCH.md](docs/RESEARCH.md). No upstream source code or pretrained weights are
bundled. The code license does not relicense third-party training data.

The six analytic animations generated by `make_procedural` are released under
[CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They are synthetic VJ-style
test loops, not artist footage or a representative VJ benchmark.

The original GLSL studies in `shaders/` and their renderer are MIT code. Their
rendered media in `artifacts/shader-preview-v0` is dedicated to CC0-1.0. The
[aesthetic research](docs/SHADER_AESTHETICS.md) cites reference artists for general
composition and motion principles; none of their media, textures or source code
is used in these renders or bundled as training data. This original shader branch
is separate from the noncommercial Electric Sheep experiments below.

The Aperture-only weights in `artifacts/aperture-fast-v1` and
`experiments/aperture-fast/trials` are MIT licensed. Their original shader target
dataset and rendered media are CC0-1.0. These runs use no Electric Sheep or public
plasma data, pretrained weights or reference-artist footage.

The public pilot includes **-PLASMA-ColorCycling.Gif**, by **Ósemkowski**, obtained from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:-PLASMA-ColorCycling.Gif),
under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Changes: center square
crop, downsample to 32×32, resample to 32 frames and retime from 5.12 seconds to two
seconds. Derived media retains its source attribution and license. The download URL
and SHA-256 are in [datasets/public-loops.json](datasets/public-loops.json). No audio is
used. The `artifacts/mixed-v0`, `artifacts/mixed-v1` and mixed-data autoresearch trial
weights/previews are released under CC BY-SA 3.0, with this attribution carried
forward. The procedural-only weights are MIT licensed.

The native 512×512 research dataset uses three **Electric Sheep** self-loops,
generation 248, serials **27561, 27681 and 27741**, by **Scott Draves and the Electric
Sheep**, from the [artist-uploaded archive](https://archive.org/details/electricsheep-flock-248-27500-1).
The archive licenses these clips under [CC BY-NC 3.0 US](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/us/).
Changes: center square crop from 800×592 to 512×512, resample 160 frames at 30 fps
to 64 frames at 12 fps, preserving the complete 5⅓-second cycle; discard audio.
The research weights and reconstructed previews in `experiments/rave-512` carry the same **noncommercial** restriction and attribution.
The MIT code license does not authorize commercial use of these media or weights.
See the [artist's reuse policy](https://electricsheep.org/license/) and
[source/checksum manifest](datasets/rave-loops.json). Output filenames retain the
required generation and serial numbers. Local downloaded originals remain outside Git.

Other artist VJ-loop sources investigated:

- [Beeple VJ loops](https://beeple-crap.com/vjloops): the artist states that the visuals
  can be used commercially and noncommercially under Creative Commons; audio has
  separate rights. Tested Vimeo/MediaFire hosts were unavailable or forbade access
  in this environment. No Beeple media was used for training.
- [Neb Motion free loops](https://nebmotion.co.uk/vj-loops/free/): geometric/tunnel packs
  are described broadly as free under Creative Commons on the page. The tested
  pack's bundled license specifically prohibits redistribution of animation files;
  it was excluded. No Neb Motion media was used for training.

Source availability and license text were checked November 3, 2026. Source pages and
licenses accompany the manifest so others can review provenance without trusting an
unexplained media archive.
