# Attribution and data provenance

The implementation in `src/vj_nca` is original code, informed by the papers linked in
[docs/RESEARCH.md](docs/RESEARCH.md). No upstream source code or pretrained weights are
bundled. The code license does not relicense third-party training data.

The six analytic animations generated by `make_procedural` are released under
[CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They are synthetic VJ-style
test loops, not artist footage or a representative VJ benchmark.

The public pilot includes **-PLASMA-ColorCycling.Gif**, by **Ósemkowski**, obtained from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:-PLASMA-ColorCycling.Gif),
under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Changes: center square
crop, downsample to 32×32, resample to 32 frames and retime from 5.12 seconds to two
seconds. Derived media retains its source attribution and license. The download URL
and SHA-256 are in [datasets/public-loops.json](datasets/public-loops.json). No audio is
used. The `artifacts/mixed-v0`, `artifacts/mixed-v1` and mixed-data autoresearch trial
weights/previews are released under CC BY-SA 3.0, with this attribution carried
forward. The procedural-only weights are MIT licensed.

Artist VJ-loop sources found during research:

- [Beeple VJ loops](https://beeple-crap.com/vjloops): the artist states that the visuals
  can be used commercially and noncommercially under Creative Commons; audio has
  separate rights. The tested individual Vimeo download returned HTTP 451 in this
  environment. No Beeple media was downloaded or used for training.
- [Neb Motion free loops](https://nebmotion.co.uk/vj-loops/free/): geometric/tunnel packs
  are described as free under Creative Commons. These remain candidate sources;
  per-pack terms and actual files must be checked before inclusion.

Source availability and license text were checked November 3, 2026. Source pages and
licenses accompany the manifest so others can review provenance without trusting an
unexplained media archive.
